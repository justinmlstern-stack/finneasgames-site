TipTapTilt

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Finneas Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how TipTapTilt handles information.

Information collected by the app

TipTapTilt does not require an account and is designed so that ordinary gameplay can occur without providing personal information to Finneas Games.

Motion and device sensors

TipTapTilt uses device motion and orientation information to control gameplay. This information is used on the device to operate the game and is not used by Finneas Games to identify you.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

TipTapTilt does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. If analytics or other data-collection features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Gameplay data

Game settings, scores, progress, or similar information may be stored locally on the device so the app can remember the player’s experience.

Contacting support

If you contact Finneas Games for support, we may receive the information included in your email, such as your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and address the issue.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if TipTapTilt changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@finneasgames.com.
