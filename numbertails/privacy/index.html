Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Finneas Games respects the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy describes how Number Tails handles information.

Information collected by the app

Number Tails does not require an account and is designed so that gameplay information and progress are stored locally on the user’s device. Finneas Games does not use Number Tails to collect personal information from children.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Number Tails does not include third-party advertising and does not use cross-app tracking. The app does not use third-party analytics to build profiles of users.

Gameplay data

Game progress, settings, and similar information may be stored locally on the device so the app can remember the player’s experience. This information is not transmitted to Finneas Games by the app.

Contacting support

If a parent or guardian contacts Finneas Games for support, we may receive the information included in that email, such as the sender’s email address and the contents of the message. We use that information only to respond to the request and address the issue.

Children’s privacy

Number Tails is designed with children and families in mind. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact us and request deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Number Tails changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to support@finneasgames.com.
